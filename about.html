About YEALMUN

YEALMUN is a Model United Nations conference organized by students at İzmir Yunus Emre Anatolian High School. Our mission is to bring together young leaders from around the world to debate, collaborate, and find solutions to global challenges.

The conference provides a unique platform for participants to develop their diplomatic skills, broaden their perspectives, and build lasting international friendships.

YEALMUN 2026 will take place on October 16-18, 2026 in İzmir Yunus Emre Anatolian High School.
